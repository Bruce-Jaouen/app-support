SUPPORT

Something unclear, broken, or worth improving? Write to Bruce at movingmass0@gmail.com.

When reporting a problem, include your device model, iOS version, app version, and what you were doing when it happened. Please do not email private health or training details.

Common questions

  • Where is my training data?Microdose Training stores plans, schedules, profile details, and completed work locally on your device. Bruce cannot view, retrieve, or restore it.
  • How do I remove my data?Open the Data Center and choose Manage Local Data. You can delete selected records or erase everything stored by the app.
  • Why did an alert not appear?Confirm that alert access is enabled in the app and in iOS settings. Lock Screen and alarm behavior should be tested on a physical iPhone.
  • Can I change a built-in exercise?Yes. Built-in exercises can be customized and later reset to their original definition.
  • How does Apple Health work?Completed training can be exported after an explicit tap. Export is optional and write-only; the app never reads your Health data.
