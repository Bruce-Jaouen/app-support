PRIVACY
POLICY

Microdose Training is designed so Bruce does not receive or maintain a copy of your training data.

Information collection

Microdose Training does not require an account and does not collect your name, email address, identifiers, location, contacts, purchases, usage analytics, advertising data, health data, or training history. The app does not contain advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

Data stored on your device

Exercises, workouts, weekly plans, schedules, active training, completion history, preferences, and optional profile details such as age, height, weight, and formula sex are stored locally in the app’s container on your device. This information is used only to provide features inside Microdose Training.

Apple Health

You may explicitly export completed training to Apple Health. Microdose Training requests permission to write workouts only and does not request permission to read Health data. Export never occurs automatically. Workouts saved to Apple Health are controlled by Apple Health and may remain there after their Microdose Training record or the app is deleted.

Alerts and Live Activities

Microdose Training can schedule training alerts and display timing information on the Lock Screen or Dynamic Island when you enable those features. These features use Apple’s on-device system frameworks and do not send training data to Bruce.

Data sharing and sale

Bruce does not sell, rent, share, or use your information for advertising because Microdose Training does not collect it.

Support email

If you contact support by email, Bruce receives the address and information you choose to include. It is used only to respond to your request. Do not send health records or other sensitive information.

Deleting data

You can remove selected categories or erase all Microdose Training data from Manage Local Data inside the app. Deleting the app also removes its local container, subject to Apple’s device backup and restoration behavior. Apple Health exports must be managed separately in Apple Health.

Children’s privacy

Microdose Training is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If the app’s privacy behavior changes, this policy will be updated before the change is released where required.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to Bruce at movingmass0@gmail.com.
