SUPPORT

Something unclear, broken, or worth improving? Write to Bruce at movingmass0@gmail.com.

When reporting a problem, include your device model, iOS version, app version, and what you were doing when it happened. Please do not email private health or training details.

Common questions

  • Where is my training data?Moving Mass stores its programs, profile, and workout history locally on your device. Bruce cannot view, retrieve, or restore it.
  • How do I delete my data?Open the Data Center, tap the settings button, then open Manage Local Data. You can clear selected categories or erase everything stored by Moving Mass.
  • Why did a rest alert not appear?Confirm that alerts are enabled from Training Now and that Moving Mass has permission to schedule alarms. Alarm behavior should be tested on a physical iPhone.
  • How does Apple Health work?After a completed workout, tap Save to Apple Health. Export is optional and write-only. Permission can be managed from Apple Health settings.
  • Does deleting a workout remove it from Apple Health?No. Once exported, that copy is managed in Apple Health and must be deleted there.
