SUPPORT

Something unclear, broken, or worth improving? Write to Bruce at movingmass0@gmail.com.

When reporting a problem, include your device model, iOS version, app version, and what you were doing when it happened. Please do not email personal or sensitive information.

Common questions

  • Where are my timers and workflows?Kitchen Flow Timer stores them locally in the app’s private container. Bruce cannot view, retrieve, or restore them.
  • Why did an alarm not sound?Open Privacy Center and confirm Alarm access is on. Also check the app’s permissions in Settings. Alarm behavior should be tested on a physical iPhone or iPad.
  • Why is voice entry unavailable?English voice entry requires microphone access, compatible hardware, and Apple’s on-device speech assets. Check microphone access in Privacy Center. For a first-use speech download, connect to Wi-Fi and try again. Manual timer creation is always available.
  • Can Bruce hear my voice entries?No. iOS processes the microphone audio on your device. Kitchen Flow Timer does not save or upload the recording or transcript, and Bruce has no way to access either one.
  • How do I change a timer?Open its actions menu and choose Edit, or use the pencil in the focused timer view. Editing resets the current run. To keep cooking a little longer, use Add 30 seconds or Add 1 minute instead.
  • What is Stopwatch?It counts up when you do not know how long something will take. Tap Finished when the task is done, then save a result or note for next time.
  • Does Kitchen Memory change my timers?No. After at least two timings marked Just Right, it can suggest a target. Nothing changes until you tap Use Next Time.
  • Can I edit built-in workflows?Yes. Your changes are stored locally. Open the customized workflow and choose Reset to Built-In whenever you want the original version back.
  • Can I use another timer during a workflow?Yes. Tap Minimize to leave the cooking view without cancelling. Use the return-to-cooking bar to reopen the same step. One workflow and multiple independent timers can run at once.
  • Where are my notes and recorded times?Open Cooking History from the Timers toolbar or Privacy Center. Tap a record to edit its note or result. Swipe a record to delete it.
  • Can I keep a backup?Open Privacy Center, then Backup & Restore, and export to Files. Restoring replaces the current saved collection, so export it first if you want to keep it. Restored timers are ready to start; finish or cancel an active workflow before restoring.
  • How do I find a workflow?Use the Library search field or tap a visible category such as Bread & Dough, Eggs & Breakfast, or Grains & Pasta.
  • How do I delete my data?Open Privacy Center, then Manage Local Data. You can delete timers, reset workflows, replay guidance, or erase everything stored by Kitchen Flow Timer.
