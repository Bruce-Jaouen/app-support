PRIVACY
POLICY

Kitchen Flow Timer is designed so Bruce does not receive or maintain a copy of your timers, workflows, timing observations, or voice audio.

Information collection

Kitchen Flow Timer does not require an account and does not collect your name, email address, identifiers, location, contacts, purchases, usage analytics, advertising data, timer history, workflow data, or voice recordings. The app contains no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs and sends no data to a developer-operated service.

Data stored on your device

Your timers, user-created workflows, customizations to built-in workflows, active workflow session, recorded cook times, notes, timing outcomes, favorites, drafts, preferences, and first-use guidance state are stored locally in the app’s private container. This information provides features inside Kitchen Flow Timer and is not available to Bruce.

Alarms and Live Activities

Kitchen Flow Timer can schedule alarms and show active timing information on the Lock Screen or Dynamic Island. These features use Apple’s on-device AlarmKit and Live Activities frameworks. Timer information is shared with iOS only as needed to present those system experiences and is not sent to Bruce.

Voice entry

Voice entry uses the microphone and Apple’s on-device speech recognition. While you speak, the app temporarily receives microphone audio so iOS can produce the timer phrase on your device. Neither the recording nor transcript is saved, uploaded, added to your timer record, or accessible to Bruce. Both disappear when you leave voice entry. Apple may require an on-device speech asset to be downloaded by iOS before this feature is available.

Data sharing and sale

Bruce does not sell, rent, share, or use your information for advertising because Kitchen Flow Timer does not collect it.

Backups and files you choose to export

You can export timers, saved workflows, favorites, notes, and cooking history as a backup file. Preserved recovery files may also be included. The backup goes only to the location or service you choose in Apple’s file picker or share sheet. If you choose iCloud Drive or another cloud provider, that provider stores the file under its own terms. Bruce does not receive a copy. Exported files remain outside the app until you delete them yourself.

Apple’s device-backup and restoration settings may also include the app’s local data. Those backups are controlled by you and Apple, not Bruce.

Shortcuts and external links

Optional App Shortcuts open the app to create a timer, open voice entry, or start a workflow. Siri and Shortcuts are Apple services governed by your system settings and Apple’s privacy policies. Opening support or privacy links uses your browser. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process normal web request information under its own privacy policy. We add no analytics or tracking scripts.

Support email

If you contact support by email, Bruce receives the address and information you choose to include. It is used only to respond to your request. Do not send sensitive information.

Deleting data

You can remove selected categories or erase all Kitchen Flow Timer data from Manage Local Data inside the app. Deleting the app also removes its local container, subject to Apple’s device backup and restoration behavior.

Children’s privacy

Kitchen Flow Timer is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If the app’s privacy behavior changes, this policy will be updated before the change is released where required.
