Built for a working kitchen

01 / Time

Every pan has a clock

Create independent timers with a name, duration, and color. Start, pause, resume, reset, and edit them without losing track of the rest.

02 / Speak

Say it and keep cooking

Say a duration and an optional name, then check the result before starting. In-app voice entry understands English and uses on-device speech recognition. Apple may download speech support before first use.

03 / Follow

Workflows that wait for you

Search 27 workflows, save favorites, or build your own. Each step waits for you to continue. Minimize a workflow to check another timer without losing your place.

04 / Make

Learn the real time

Use the stopwatch for a first attempt, or mark a timed task finished when the food is ready. Keep the time, result, and a note in Cooking History. After repeated cooks marked Just Right, you can choose to reuse a suggested time.

Privacy receipt

YOUR KITCHEN RECORD STAYS YOURS.

Kitchen Flow Timer has no account system, analytics SDK, advertising network, or developer-operated backend. Your timers, workflows, and timing observations are stored on your device.

Alarm, Lock Screen, Dynamic Island, microphone, and on-device speech features use Apple’s system frameworks. Bruce does not receive your timer data or voice audio.

Export a backup to a location you choose or erase your records in the Privacy Center. Apple’s device-backup settings may also include app data.

Made for iPhone and iPad. Developed by Bruce. Cooking times are guides, not a test of doneness; check the food and use a thermometer where appropriate.
